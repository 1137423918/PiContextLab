解释（待核实）

Dataset ds_1f8144bb8670bcad07cde082 covers 2010-12-01 to 2011-12-09, so both requested calendar months are fully inside coverage; no partial-period caveat applies. Policy retail-gbp-v1 defines returns as the absolute amount of eligible negative-quantity lines, including cancellations, in GBP; it is a bookkeeping measure, not cash refunded. Per the catalog, duplicate source lines are preserved and return adjustments remain in source, so figures are descriptive only. Comparison (ev_cbd6a81c...): returns rose from the April 2011 baseline to the May 2011 current period, a positive delta. All quantitative values are in the evidence record. Country breakdown, top 8 plus OTHER (ev_ee979276...): the increase is not broad-based. The United Kingdom is the single largest positive contributor and by itself exceeds the total net change, meaning other countries net out negative. Singapore and Japan show returns in the baseline and none in the current month, and France, Australia and Switzerland also declined; these are the main offsets. Portugal and Channel Islands show no baseline returns and new current-period returns. The OTHER bucket (23 remaining countries) is small and slightly negative. Top-8 plus OTHER reconciles exactly to the comparison totals, so no unexplained residual exists. Interpretation limits: the breakdown is additive and descriptive. The concentration in the United Kingdom and the appearance/disappearance of returns in specific countries are consistent with a small number of large return or cancellation events rather than a shift in underlying return propensity, but this dataset view cannot distinguish those. No causal claim is made.

待验证假设

  • A small number of large-value return or cancellation lines in the United Kingdom drove most of the month-over-month increase, rather than a broad rise in return frequency.
  • The Singapore and Japan baseline returns with zero current-month returns reflect one-off cancellation events in April rather than a durable improvement in those markets.
  • The new Portugal and Channel Islands current-month returns reflect isolated large adjustments rather than newly established return activity.
  • Because returns include cancellation invoices, part of the movement may reflect invoice-timing/bookkeeping effects (when cancellations were recorded) rather than changes in customer return behavior.
